# Africa — Savanna Village V0.5

## Visual target
A polished, colorful Roblox-style savanna settlement with rounded acacia canopies, detailed huts, market stalls, sculpted rocks, warm sunlight, a central plaza, and a stream.

## Prototype footprint
600 × 600 studs. Establish a walkable village center and surrounding exploration trails.

## Gameplay
NPC quests, gathering, mining, crafting, and XP. No mandatory dungeon.

## Build stages
1. Create Blender asset prototypes and render them for quality review.
2. Optimize and export meshes.
3. Import into a backed-up Roblox Studio place.
4. Build gameplay interactions and test on desktop and mobile.

## Safety
Do not overwrite the existing continents, levels, relics, or quest data. Avoid claiming visual parity with concept art until actual in-engine screenshots are reviewed.
